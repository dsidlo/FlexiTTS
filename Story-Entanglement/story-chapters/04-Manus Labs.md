## - Manus Labs -

As the bunker locks re-engaged, sealing the deal both literally and metaphorically, Yamo couldn't shake the feeling that he had just crossed a line, stepped into a shadowy realm where the stakes were higher and the rules were written in a language he didn't fully understand. But in a world on the brink, sometimes the only way to survive was to dance with the devil. And so they danced.

The limo glided through the neon tapestry of the city, its electric hum drowned out by the cacophony of urban life. They merged onto the highway, the skyline receding in the rearview mirror as they sped toward the enigmatic fortress of Manus Labs. When they arrived, the parking lot was bathed in the yellow glow of mercury vapor lights, relics from a bygone era that cast long, distorted shadows on the asphalt.

"Clear,"
Juko's voice crackled through the limo's internal comms. "You can exit the vehicle."

No sooner had they stepped onto the lot than the air itself seemed to ripple, to fracture like a broken mirror. Six figures materialized out of the distortion, their movements so fast they seemed to rez in from hyper-motion. Yakuza assassins, augmented to the point of being post-human, surrounded the group in a deadly semicircle.

"Payment delivered," 
the one facing Hendrix sneered. 
"But we weren't told to stand down. Someone's not happy with you."

"That's unfortunate," 
the Yakuza talker grinned, 
"because we're about to have some collateral damage."

Almost simultaneously, Juko's augments activated. Two glowing threads of mono-molecular wire flashed from his fingertips, slicing through the air—and through two more assassins—with surgical precision. They fell, their bodies partitioned into grotesque sculptures of flesh and bone.

Hendrix' mantis blades deployed in a flash of cold steel, skewering the talker even as he lunged at two others attempting to retreat. With a feral leap, he bisected one mid-air, landing in a crouch as the two halves of the man fell to the ground.

As the dust settled, as the echoes of violence faded into the night, the group stood in a circle, their faces lit by the flickering mercury vapor lights. They were a motley crew of scientists, drivers, and augmented warriors, bound by a web of secrets and debts, of loyalties and betrayals.

But in that moment, as they looked at each other across the circle of fallen bodies, they knew that the game had changed. The stakes had been raised, and the rules rewritten. And as they turned to face the looming edifice of Manus Labs, its glass façade reflecting the chaos that had just unfolded, they knew that they were stepping into a new chapter, a new level of the game.

And this time, they were playing for keeps.
